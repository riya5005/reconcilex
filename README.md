# ReconcileX

### AI-assisted Payment Reconciliation & Resolution

ReconcileX helps payment operations teams identify suspicious payment situations, understand what happened, and safely resolve them.

## The problem

A customer may accidentally get charged twice for the same order.

For example:

```text
Order value     ₹2,500
Payment 1       ₹2,500
Payment 2       ₹2,500

Collected       ₹5,000
Expected        ₹2,500
```
## Our approach

Payment Data

     ↓
Duplicate Detection

     ↓
Evidence & Confidence

     ↓
AI Investigation

     ↓
Decision
     ↓
Human Approval
     ↓
Resolution
     ↓
Ledger + Audit Trail
